Step 01

Research: Apps for Other Sports

First, we researched popular apps for land and water sports to list their main functions.

Sport Common App Functions Limitation for Sailing
Running Measures distance, step count, and running pace. Does not measure wind or water currents.
Cycling Shows road maps, elevation, and bike speed. There are no roads or fixed lines on the sea.
Swimming Counts pool laps and measures stroke time. Only works inside a swimming pool, not open water.

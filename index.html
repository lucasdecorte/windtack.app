2. Our Sport: Dinghy Sailing (ILCA Class)

The ILCA is a single-handed Olympic sailboat. It is very popular in yacht clubs in our country.

01 / Target Users

Who will use the app?

  • Youth sailors who train in ILCA 4 and ILCA 6 boats.
  • Sailing club students learning how to tack and gybe.
  • Sailing coaches watching the fleet from motorboats.
02 / Measurements

What does the app measure?

  • Boat speed in knots (nautical miles per hour).
  • Wind direction and wind shifts on the water.
  • Tack angles and total sailing distance.
03 / Interaction

How do people interact?

  • Club Fleet Map: See where your teammates are sailing.
  • Quick Signals: Send wind alerts before the race starts.
  • Track Replay: Compare your route with friends after training.
04 / Advantages

Why is it better and more fun?

  • General weather apps do not show boat speed or tack angles.
  • WindTack is more useful because it is made for ILCA boats.
  • It is more fun because you race your friends' Ghost Boats and win club badges.

How WindTack Works

1. Sail Screen (Telemetry)

Shows boat speed in knots, compass heading, and your current tack (Starboard or Port) in big numbers.

2. Club Screen (Teamwork)

Shows your yacht club teammates on the water and lets you send quick wind shift alerts before a race.

3. Races Screen (Ghost Race)

Saves your sailing distance, lets you race against a friend's Ghost Boat, and gives you club badges.
